- Modernized and established as the official umbrella package for the entire Widgetarian ecosystem.
- Exports all modular packages: `animated_checkmark`, `animated_icon_theme`, `animated_repeatable`, `animated_switcher_transitions`, `animated_transform`, `choice`, `chronograph`, `disclosure`, `fancy_border`, `lerp`, `marquee_transition`, `overlap_stack`, `theme_patrol`, `wheel_choice`, `wheel_choice_ext`, `wx_avatar`, `wx_badge`, `wx_box`, `wx_divider`, `wx_layout`, `wx_spoiler`, `wx_text`, `wx_text_animated`, `wx_tile`, and `wx_utils`.
- Dart 3 workspace ready and aligned with Flutter 3.24+.
